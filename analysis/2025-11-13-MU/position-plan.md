**Phase 1: Initial Entry (Until +5% profit)**
- **Stop**: $217.00 (firm)

**Phase 2: Breakeven (After +5% profit)**

**Phase 3: Profit Protection (After Target 1 hit)**
- **Trigger**: Price reaches $248
- **New Stop**: $240.00
- **Locked Profit**: Minimum +3% to +7% depending on entry

**Phase 4: Trailing (After Target 2 hit)**
- **Trigger**: Price reaches $257
- **New Stop**: $248.00
- **Trail Method**: Maintain $8-10 behind price as it advances
- **Final Position**: Trail with MA(20) or -12% from peak

### Stop Loss Discipline

**Rules**:
1. NEVER widen stop loss (only tighten or maintain)
2. NEVER remove stop loss ("hope" is not a strategy)
3. NEVER average down after stop is hit
4. Execute stop immediately on break, no hesitation
5. If stopped out, wait 48 hours before re-entry consideration

**Mental Stop vs. Hard Stop**:
- **Recommended**: Hard stop (GTC stop-loss order at $216.50)
