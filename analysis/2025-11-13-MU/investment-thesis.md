### Bear Case (25% probability): Cyclical Peak, Margin Compression

**Scenario**: Memory cycle peaks in 2026, industry capacity additions create oversupply, margins compress

- Gross margins compress to 28-32%, operating margins 10-15%
- CapEx remains elevated at $12-14B (technology race continues)

**Return**: -40% to -70% from current price ($233 → $60-140)
